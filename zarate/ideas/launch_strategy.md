# Zárate Joyería — Launch Strategy

Last updated from Claude.ai memory export (May 2026)

## Core Blockers Identified
1. No payment system
2. No product photography setup
3. Tight budget (under $50/month)

## Recommended Approach
- Use existing **GoDaddy site as portfolio** with WhatsApp contact buttons
- Free Colombian payment methods: **Nequi, Daviplata, bank transfer**
- Focus products: **bracelets, diamond pendants, studs**
- **Made-to-order model**

## Launch Plan
- Full brand launch strategy covering **9 sequential phases** (detailed in Notion)
- iPhone-based content plan
- Low-budget launch roadmap
- All organized as nested Notion pages

## Brand Positioning
- Premium and artisanal tone
- Target: Medellín luxury audience
- Spanish-language content
- Handmade pieces in 18k gold with Colombian emeralds, diamonds, sapphires, rubies

## Business Model
- Small B2B jewelry manufacturing workshop in Medellín
- Transitioning to own fine jewelry brand (B2C)
